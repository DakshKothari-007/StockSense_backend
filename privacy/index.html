Privacy Policy

Plain-English explanation of how your store data is collected, used, and protected.

Effective: November 2026 Last Updated: 21 November 2026
Digital Personal Data Protection: StockSense complies with the principles of India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 to protect your retail data and personal information.

1. What Data We Collect

To help you run and manage your supermarket or grocery store, we collect only the information necessary for store operations:

  • Account details: Store name, owner name, email address, and password (stored solely as a secure BCrypt cryptographic hash, never in plaintext).
  • Inventory & products: Product names, categories, selling prices, purchase costs, inventory counts (stock units), barcode/SKUs, and minimum warning thresholds.
  • Sales transactions: Sale bill amounts, sold items, quantities, transaction dates, and times.
  • Purchase orders: Restock orders, ordered quantities, estimated costs, and delivery statuses.
  • Device identifiers: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device push tokens to deliver operational low-stock and order alerts to your mobile device.

2. Why We Collect It

We use your store data exclusively to provide and improve core StockSense services:

  • Tracking live stock levels and alerting you before products sell out.
  • Fast point-of-sale (POS) billing with real-time stock deduction.
  • Generating intelligent reorder recommendations and purchase order tracking.
  • Delivering weekly performance email digests to review revenue and slow-moving items.
  • Providing grounded answers and retail analytics through our Dukaan Mitra assistant.

3. AI Assistant (Dukaan Mitra)

StockSense includes Dukaan Mitra, an AI co-pilot designed for grocery and supermarket operations.

  • How it works: When you ask Dukaan Mitra a question, your query and relevant shop numbers (such as sales figures or low-stock items) are processed by our hybrid AI engine. The engine runs locally on our private server using Ollama, with Google Gemini used for general knowledge questions.
  • Cloud Privacy: Shop metrics and sales counts are kept strictly local and are never shared with cloud models for marketing or training.
Please Note: While Dukaan Mitra uses real verified database numbers for store queries, AI assistants can occasionally make mistakes or interpret conversational requests unexpectedly. Please verify critical financial, tax, or supplier decisions independently.

4. Who Sees Your Data

Only you. StockSense enforces strict multi-tenant isolation. Every database query is cryptographically bound to your authenticated shop owner session.

  • No other shop owner or store can ever view, search, or access your products, prices, or sales.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your data to advertisers, aggregators, or third parties.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

We partner with trusted service providers strictly to perform essential app infrastructure functions:

  • Firebase (Google LLC): Dispatches push notification alerts (low-stock warnings and purchase order updates) directly to your device via the FCM HTTP v1 protocol.
  • Google Gemini (Google LLC): Processes general business or informational questions when cloud AI is active. (Store inventory and financial numbers remain strictly local).
  • Email Provider (SMTP): Delivers your automated Sunday weekly store digest.

These providers process data strictly to fulfill their operational roles and are prohibited from using your data for secondary purposes.

6. Security & Storage

We protect your information with strong technical safeguards:

  • Session tokens: 64-character hexadecimal Bearer tokens stored as SHA-256 hashes with automatic expiration.
  • Passwords: One-way BCrypt hashing with failed attempt rate-limiting protection.
  • Encryption in transit: HTTPS encryption once deployed to production.
  • Isolated storage: Private API keys and Firebase service credentials reside outside the public web root.

7. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Your data stays with StockSense only for as long as your account remains active.

Account Deletion: You can permanently delete your entire account and all associated store data at any time directly inside the app under Settings > Delete Account.

When you confirm account deletion, the system performs an immediate atomic deletion across all products, sales records, purchase orders, device tokens, and settings. Nothing is retained.
